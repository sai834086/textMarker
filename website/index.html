How it works

Start it, then just read. No pasting text, no setup.

Read aloud

Click Start and read from whatever's on your screen. Speech recognition runs locally — offline and private.

It highlights your line

textMarker matches your words to the text on screen and highlights the line and word you're on.

It scrolls with you

As you reach the bottom, it gently auto-scrolls — and keeps searching for the next block of text so you never lose your place.

Why it's different

It lives in the gap between teleprompters and text-to-speech readers.

Voice teleprompters
Follow your voice — but only text pasted into their own app.
Text-to-speech readers
Highlight as the computer reads to you — not as you read.

What's inside

  • Any app: browser, PDF, Notepad, docs — reads what's on screen.
  • Follows your pace: highlight moves as fast or slow as you talk.
  • Smart auto-scroll: scrolls at the bottom and hunts for the next text.
  • Reading region: draw a box to track just the part you care about.
  • Offline & private: speech never leaves your computer.
  • One-click install: desktop app with a Start-menu shortcut.
